# Echo Clinical Review Pack — Stage 4

**Governance state for every rule:** `CLINICAL_REVIEW`  
**Execution state:** blocked; no rule is published  
**Implementation version:** 1  
**Review requirement:** licensed source extraction, cardiologist review, separate technical test evidence, approval and publication

| Rule ID                          | Clinical question / result                                                       | Inputs and exclusions                                                                                                 | Evidence                                      | Boundary tests                                                               |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `echo.measurement-quality`       | Which source may guide current decisions? Produces a reviewed source preference. | Type, formality, quality, method, provenance; exclude entered-in-error sources.                                       | ASE reporting 2025                            | Good formal study; newer limited bedside study; clinician override           |
| `echo.lv-chamber-classification` | How should LV size and systolic function be classified?                          | Dimensions/volumes, indexing, LVEF method and quality; require population and measurement context.                    | ASE/EACVI chambers 2015; ASE reporting 2025   | Quantitative study; missing BSA; visual estimate                             |
| `echo.rv-integrated-assessment`  | What integrated RV state is supported?                                           | Dimensions, FAC, TAPSE, S′, strain, 3D EF and quality; do not rely on one metric.                                     | ASE right heart 2025                          | Concordant normal; discordant metrics; limited study                         |
| `echo.diastolic-function`        | Can diastolic state be classified transparently?                                 | Inflow, e′, E/e′, TR velocity, LA volume, rhythm and special population; exclude unsupported/out-of-scope algorithms. | ASE diastology 2025                           | Normal; abnormal; indeterminate; unable; AF                                  |
| `echo.pulmonary-pressure`        | What Echo probability/context is supported?                                      | TR velocity, RA pressure, RVSP/PASP and RV/PA/IVC signs; never diagnose PH from one estimate.                         | ASE right heart 2025                          | Complete signs; single estimate; unmeasurable TR                             |
| `echo.aortic-dimension`          | Does aortic size need reviewed classification or surveillance?                   | Level, method, diameter, indexing and prior; flag incomparable methods or missing body size.                          | ASE reporting 2025                            | Stable; changed; different method; missing body size                         |
| `echo.change-lvef`               | Does interval LV function change require reassessment?                           | Current/prior LVEF, method, quality and interval; withhold numeric significance until reviewed.                       | ASE reporting 2025                            | Decline; improvement; method discordance; poor new source                    |
| `echo.change-valve`              | Has a clinician-confirmed valve lesion progressed or improved?                   | Current/prior integrated finding, source and quality; exclude incomparable or indeterminate sources.                  | ASE reporting 2025; native regurgitation 2017 | Progression; improvement; discordance                                        |
| `echo.prosthetic-change`         | Does a prosthetic study differ from baseline enough to need review?              | Known baseline, current/previous measurements, regurgitation, quality; no automatic mechanism diagnosis.              | ASE prosthetic valve 2024                     | Stable baseline; changed gradient; new leak; absent baseline                 |
| `echo.internal-consistency`      | Which factual inconsistencies or omissions need reporter review?                 | Finalization, measurements, units, linked findings and conclusion; checks documentation, not diagnosis.               | ASE reporting 2025                            | Missing conclusion; severe lesion without support; discordance; missing LVEF |

Class/level fields remain unset unless the independently reviewed source explicitly supports their use. Rule definitions and test fixtures are available from `/api/echo-valve/review-pack?domain=echo`.
