# AF ablation and LAA closure: deeper evidence review

Research date: 10 October 2026. Scope: the existing AF procedure module, including preparation, aftercare, antithrombotics, surveillance and complications. Findings below distinguish clinical guidelines, expert statements, device labeling and randomized trials. This is a targeted evidence review, not a registered systematic review. The verified adjustments described below are implemented on the AF feature branch; production deployment remains pending.

## Search and verification

PubMed search: `("atrial fibrillation"[Title] OR "left atrial appendage"[Title]) AND (guideline[Title] OR consensus[Title] OR "scientific statement"[Title] OR corrigendum[Title])`, publication dates 1 January 2025–10 October 2026, newest first. Both pages were retrieved: 60 records screened by title for relevance. Separate DOI/PMID searches retrieved the major procedure trials, older foundational guidance, and the 2026 ACC anticoagulation statement. This search does not capture every relevant document: the DOAC statement, for example, needs a separate query because its title lacks AF/LAA.

Society sites, publisher pages, author institutional records, manufacturer eIFU pages and FDA PMA supplements were searched separately. Relevant ESC recommendation-table text was retrieved from the guideline PDF hosted by the Polish Cardiac Society. The complete 2026 PFA statement was retrieved through rights-eligible PMC text in three consecutive chunks. LAAO correction wording was verified in an author's university publication record and corroborated by indexed PMC text; the publisher correction itself remained unavailable. Trial conclusions below were checked against primary publication abstracts in PubMed.

## Device labeling changes that matter

**WATCHMAN FLX Pro:** the retrieved US IFU is [52212467-01A, ©2025](https://www.bostonscientific.com/content/dam/elabeling/ic/watchman_flx_pro/52212467-01A_WATCHMAN_FLX_PRO_IFU_EN_s.pdf), pages 28–31. It separates short-term OAC, DAPT-only, and post-AF-ablation OAC pathways. The first two use 45-day assessment; the postablation option uses 3-month TEE/CT. Twelve-month imaging is also specified. The label includes device-specific leak/thrombus criteria and six-month endocarditis-prophylaxis advice. These are clinician-reviewed protocol options, not automatic prescriptions. A statement that there is no universal leak cutoff must not hide a cutoff in an applicable device IFU. Figure 11 was visually checked. The manufacturer identifies July 2025 labeling updates based on OPTION/SURPASS; exact regional applicability must be recorded.

**Amplatzer Amulet:** FDA [S012, approved 18 September 2025](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?ID=P200049S012), updates postimplant medication instructions to include OAC and removes a late-effusion warning. [S021, approved 28 August 2026](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?ID=P200049S021), changes a contraindication and adds landing-zone/pulmonary-artery relationship assessment to reduce injury. These approvals are verified; the complete updated country-specific IFU was not retrieved. The [original 2021 FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf20/P200049C.pdf) is historical and cannot justify a current universal “DAPT only/no OAC” rule. Retrieve the exact device/model, jurisdiction and revision from [Abbott eLabeling](https://manuals.eifu.abbott/en/index.html) before creating regimen presets.

**Amulet 360:** [Abbott announced CE Mark on 27 August 2026](https://abbott.mediaroom.com/2026-08-27-Abbotts-next-generation-Amulet-360-TM-device-receives-CE-Mark-to-treat-people-with-atrial-fibrillation-at-risk-of-stroke). Treat it as a distinct model; CE Mark does not establish US approval or permission to reuse the original Amulet protocol. Its current regional IFU remains a separate verification task.

## New trials: important, but not interchangeable with guidelines

| Primary trial | Verified finding and limits | App implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OPTION](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39555822/), 1,600 postablation patients | At 36 months, nonprocedural major/clinically relevant nonmajor bleeding: 8.5% with closure vs 18.1% with OAC. Death/stroke/systemic embolism: 5.3% vs 5.8%, meeting noninferiority. Device/procedure complications occurred in 23 patients. | Supports discussing postablation closure in matching patients, including its procedural risks and early therapy. |
| [CHAMPION-AF](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41910347/), 3,000 OAC-eligible patients | Three-year cardiovascular death/stroke/systemic embolism: 5.7% closure vs 4.8% NOAC, meeting prespecified noninferiority. Nonprocedural bleeding: 10.9% vs 19.0%. | A shared-decision evidence update, not proof of better stroke prevention or a universal closure-first strategy. |
| [CLOSURE-AF](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41849741/), 912 higher-risk patients | Older population, mean CHA₂DS₂-VASc 5.2. Closure did not meet noninferiority versus best medical care for stroke/systemic embolism/major bleeding/cardiovascular or unexplained death. Primary-event rates: 16.8 vs 13.3 per 100 patient-years. | Do not equate high bleeding risk or frailty with automatic LAAO benefit. Discuss competing risks and procedural burden. |
| [ALONE-AF](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40886309/), 840 patients | No documented recurrence for ≥1 year; mean CHA₂DS₂-VASc 2.1. Two-year stroke/systemic embolism/major bleeding: 0.3% stopping vs 2.2% continuing DOAC. Few stroke events and selected population limit generalization. | Supports a documented specialist discussion in suitable patients, not immediate postablation withdrawal or extrapolation to all high-risk patients. |
| [OCEAN](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41211931/), 1,284 patients | ≥1 year after successful ablation. Rivaroxaban versus aspirin did not significantly reduce stroke/systemic embolism/new large covert infarction; relative risk 0.56, 95% CI 0.19–1.65. | Lack of significance is not proof of equivalence. The trial does not make aspirin a general substitute for OAC in AF. |

## Recommended app adjustments, in order

1. **Record the governing device protocol:** device/model, jurisdiction, IFU revision/link, selected regimen and responsible implant team. Existing aftercare records device and regimen but does not encode this full provenance.
2. **Separate combined ablation + closure care:** keep both actual procedure dates; identify which device-label option applies; offer its surveillance milestones for clinician confirmation. A generic 45–90-day note alone does not capture all current WATCHMAN options.
3. **Strengthen long-term OAC review:** distinguish the minimum recovery period from later risk-based decisions. Surface low/intermediate/high-risk consensus options, recurrence evidence, other OAC indications and a dated rhythm-monitoring/restart plan.
4. **Add the missing procedure-specific safety checks:** ablation energy/platform, PFA renal/hemolysis or ischemic symptoms, CIED interrogation when applicable, and Amulet anatomy clearance. No arbitrary laboratory cutoffs or procedural dosing should be introduced.
5. **Complete device aftercare milestones:** early imaging, later surveillance when specified, DRT resolution/repeat imaging, leak review, and applicable endocarditis-prevention counseling. Keep drug transitions explicitly clinician-confirmed.
6. **Make evidence visible in each decision:** source date/type, recommendation strength, device applicability and known gaps. Update the LAAO selection citation with the verified correction.

Current pathways already support dated review, imaging-linked decisions, DRT/leak escalation, and separate surgical/transcatheter closure. The implementation status below separates completed changes from remaining provenance and source-verification work. No new daily admission-review module is needed.

## Implementation status (10 October 2026)

- ESC is preselected for ablation minimums and LAAO eligibility. Saved assessments show the chosen reference. ESC contraindication-based LAAO eligibility and the broader, corrected SCAI conditional recommendation remain distinct.
- Recorded ablation energy drives PFA recovery advice, including renal/haemolysis/vasospasm review and CIED interrogation when applicable. Long-term OAC review shows ESC risk and EHRA withdrawal considerations while preserving the minimum period, other indications and clinician-only medication changes.
- Closure aftercare requires confirmation of a current regional/individualized protocol or an explicit unconfirmed state. Three verified US FLX Pro IFU options are available with the document revision shown; server validation rejects other devices, mismatched regimens and a postablation option without a recorded preceding AF ablation. Amulet labeling-update caveats remain visible.
- Optional 12-month surveillance creates a separate dated task that early imaging cannot close. Device-specific early-surveillance timing, leak/thrombus criteria and applicable prophylaxis counseling appear in the reviewed assessment. Dates and treatment transitions remain clinician-confirmed.
- Full structured provenance for individualized regional protocols (revision/link and responsible team), platform-specific PFA details, and complete regional Amulet/Amulet 360/FLX presets remain future work. US FLX Pro options must not be reused for those devices or assumed valid in another jurisdiction.
