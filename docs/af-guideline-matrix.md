# AF procedure care: evidence and implementation

Checked 10 October 2026. This extends the existing AF-CARE, DOAC label checks and cardioversion support. It is decision support for a cardiologist and implant/EP team, not an autonomous procedure or drug protocol.

The subsequent [deeper evidence review](af-guideline-deep-review.md) adds 2026 PFA guidance, verified LAAO correction wording, current device-label changes, five primary trials and remaining implementation gaps. Its recommendations are research findings, not deployed changes.

## Current evidence and verification limits

- ESC lists the 2024 AF guideline as the current full AF guideline. The 2024 ablation consensus and 2025 LAAO recommendations were checked against rights-eligible PMC body text, including the relevant sections. The 2023 LAAC summary recommendations were also retrieved and checked. These references do not prescribe every individual device's current IFU.
- The parallel [JSCAI corrigendum](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41726062/) (DOI 10.1016/j.jscai.2025.104164) was subsequently checked against [the correction text in an author's institutional record](https://cris.tau.ac.il/en/publications/corrigendum-to-2025-scaihrs-clinical-practice-guidelines-on-trans/): recommendation 2.1 is conditional, with moderate-certainty evidence. The separate [Heart Rhythm corrigendum](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42790981/) remains full-text unverified. Neither correction supplies a device-specific drug regimen.
- The [2026 ACC scientific statement on DOAC management](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42377292/) (DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2026.05.033) and 2026 AF quality/performance measures (DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2026.05.032) were identified. Publisher access prevented full recommendation-by-recommendation verification of the statement. Recent ablation/closure trials therefore do not become a new blanket instruction to stop anticoagulation. This limitation remains explicit instead of labelling an inferred protocol as verified 2026 guidance.
- Current manufacturer IFU and the implant team's documented protocol must be confirmed at treatment selection. Only the verified US WATCHMAN FLX Pro IFU has explicit protocol options. WATCHMAN FLX, Amplatzer Amulet, other transcatheter devices and surgical techniques must follow their own reviewed regional instructions; no automatic drug schedule is prescribed. Surgical exclusion remains generally adjunctive to indicated OAC. Other OAC indications must be reviewed independently.
- The deeper review retrieved the US WATCHMAN FLX Pro IFU 52212467-01A and verified FDA Amulet labeling supplements S012 (2025 medication update) and S021 (August 2026 anatomy safety update). The AF feature branch now offers clinician-confirmed US FLX Pro protocol options and optional 12-month surveillance; exact device/regimen applicability is server-validated. Other regional/device protocols require explicit implant-team confirmation. Complete current regional Amulet labeling remains to be retrieved.

## Workflow and record integrity

Four pathways use the existing wizard engine: ablation shared decision/preparation, actual-ablation recovery, LAA closure eligibility, and actual-closure aftercare. They work in clinic and admission contexts and write the same decision history and dated plans used by notes/discharge.

LAA imaging records purpose, TEE/CT modality, LA/LAA thrombus, device thrombus and residual leak explicitly. Missing findings never mean absent. Preclosure imaging cannot satisfy a postclosure surveillance task. Known present/indeterminate thrombus blocks elective scheduling even if a conflicting answer claims clearance. AF-CARE recognises actual transcatheter closure and does not suggest stopping device-regimen aspirin as generic AF monotherapy; post-ablation minimum periods and other anticoagulation indications remain independent. Corrections preserve history and reopen tasks linked to withdrawn evidence. Aftercare is anchored server-side to procedure/imaging IDs; a repeat procedure or a new scan cannot inherit an old clearance.

Emergency symptoms direct urgent assessment. The blanking period is a recurrence interpretation aid, not permission to ignore symptoms. No acute drug dosing, procedural energy/lesion prescription, automatic bridging, or medication transition is introduced.

ESC is the preselected reference for LAAO eligibility and post-ablation minimums. LAAO scheduling under ESC requires long-term OAC contraindication; the broader corrected SCAI shared-decision option is explicitly selectable. PFA advice reads actual procedure energy (2026 statement, DOI 10.1093/europace/euag080). Risk-based OAC review creates a review task, never a medication stop. Missing/unconfirmed closure protocols remain actionable despite clear imaging. Twelve-month imaging has an independent completion boundary. See the deep review for source and regional-label gaps.
