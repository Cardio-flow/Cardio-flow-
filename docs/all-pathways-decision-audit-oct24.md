# Catalogue-wide decision audit — 24 October 2026

Scope: every registered pathway, not only hyperkalaemia.

The shared renderer prioritises prescriptions and management, explicitly shows continue/no-change decisions, and folds exact routine prevention templates into one expandable section. Guideline dependencies are re-evaluated after clinical edits; hidden branch answers cannot contribute outcomes. Duplicate dated tasks and invalid preview dates are handled consistently. Applying the recommended plan excludes alternatives.

Specific corrections: missing symptoms are not asymptomatic hypotension; unknown INR direction does not recommend dose increase; absent lactate does not establish SCAI B; absent organ-damage screening does not establish uncomplicated severe hypertension; absent PAH risk/comorbidity data does not select combination treatment; missing HCM therapy/symptoms does not trigger beta-blocker escalation; absent HHS diagnostic criteria does not establish HHS. Congestion requires positive findings and a current red-flag assessment before an outpatient loop increase.

Resistant hypertension: after verified out-of-office hypertension on the core triple regimen, adherence and maximum tolerated doses, spironolactone 25 mg daily is proposed only with recorded eGFR >=30 and potassium <=4.5 and no current MRA/potassium-sparing drug. The existing medication safety checker still runs before confirmation. Renal/potassium review is proposed at 1 and 4 weeks (ESC HF practical MRA monitoring); 30-day result freshness follows the existing acute-medication recording convention. Referral to AF ablation/LAAO asks for the referral decision; procedure clearance remains required before scheduling. No server required-field validation was removed.

## Verification scope

`tests/all-pathways-decision.test.ts` exercises sparse and measured synthetic records and each selectable option for every registered pathway. It checks dates, duplicate tasks, supported prescription codes/doses, rendering, prioritisation and shared validation, plus targeted clinical regressions. This is catalogue-wide software coverage, not a claim that every possible clinical combination or specialist dosing regimen has been independently validated. Existing family-specific clinical and kernel tests remain necessary.

Drug/procedure choices requiring specialist protocol, measured anatomy or additional facts remain explicit assessments rather than invented automatic prescriptions. The source of the existing clinical rules is retained; this audit does not claim a new literature review of every guideline.
