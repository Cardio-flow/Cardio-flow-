# Valve Clinical Review Pack — Stage 4

**Primary source:** 2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines for valvular heart disease  
**Governance state for every rule:** `CLINICAL_REVIEW`  
**Execution state:** blocked; forms record clinician assessments only  
**Implementation version:** 1

| Rule ID                          | Clinical question / result                                             | Inputs and exclusions                                                                                                                                                | Supporting evidence                       | Boundary tests                                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `valve.as-severity`              | What multiparametric AS state is supported?                            | Morphology, Vmax, gradient, AVA/indexed AVA, DVI, flow, EF, BP and quality; no one-value classification.                                                             | ESC/EACTS 2025; ASE reporting 2025        | Concordant; discordant; low flow; uncertain measurement                                |
| `valve.as-discordant`            | What confirmation pathway is needed for discordant AS?                 | AVA/gradient/flow/EF/BP, acquisition quality and multimodality context.                                                                                              | ESC/EACTS 2025                            | AVA-gradient discordance; low flow; technical limitation                               |
| `valve.as-intervention`          | Is there a potential AS intervention trigger requiring review?         | Confirmed severity, structured symptoms, EF, exercise/risk markers, age, anatomy, comorbidity, frailty and preference; severe does not equal automatic intervention. | ESC/EACTS 2025                            | Symptomatic; symptoms unknown; asymptomatic risk marker; missing severity confirmation |
| `valve.ar-severity-intervention` | How should AR severity and timing be reviewed?                         | Integrated regurgitation, LV response, EF, aorta and symptoms.                                                                                                       | ESC/EACTS 2025; native regurgitation 2017 | Severe with LV change; asymptomatic; discordant                                        |
| `valve.primary-mr`               | How should primary MR severity and intervention timing be reviewed?    | Mechanism, integrated quantification, LV/LA response, pressure, AF and symptoms.                                                                                     | ESC/EACTS 2025; native regurgitation 2017 | Symptomatic; progressive LV change; new AF/pressure; unknown symptoms                  |
| `valve.secondary-mr`             | Is secondary MR assessment integrated with HF treatment?               | Shared HF symptoms, LVEF/LV size, GDMT, CRT/device, coronary context, pressure and RV; do not duplicate HF data.                                                     | ESC/EACTS 2025                            | Therapy not optimized; CRT context; persistent severe MR; missing HF data              |
| `valve.mitral-stenosis`          | What MS assessment and procedural factors require review?              | Morphology, area, gradient, heart rate, rhythm, pressure, symptoms and anatomical suitability.                                                                       | ESC/EACTS 2025                            | Rate contextualized; missing rate; suitability factors                                 |
| `valve.tricuspid-regurgitation`  | What integrated TR state and timing factors are present?               | Mechanism, severity, RV/RA, annulus, hepatic flow, pressure, right-HF state, leads and left-sided disease.                                                           | ESC/EACTS 2025; native regurgitation 2017 | Severe with RV dysfunction; lead-related; left-sided intervention; advanced RV disease |
| `valve.multiple-disease`         | What combined state avoids disconnected lesion plans?                  | All lesions, dominant physiology, interactions, uncertainty and Heart Team context.                                                                                  | ESC/EACTS 2025                            | AS+MR; mixed lesions; uncertain dominant lesion                                        |
| `valve.surveillance`             | What clinician-confirmed surveillance dates and early triggers apply?  | Lesion/state, ventricular response, symptoms, prior rate of change and guideline interval; interval cannot execute before review.                                    | ESC/EACTS 2025                            | Exact accepted dates; new symptoms; new AF/HF; repeat Echo supersession                |
| `valve.heart-team`               | When should Heart Team workflow be considered or advanced?             | Severity confirmation, intervention context, procedure-strategy factors and uncertainty.                                                                             | ESC/EACTS 2025                            | Consider; requested; reviewed; decision documented                                     |
| `valve.prosthetic-surveillance`  | Does prosthetic/post-intervention follow-up require additional review? | Valve record, known baseline, current/previous imaging, regurgitation, complications and symptoms; no automatic thrombosis/deterioration diagnosis.                  | ESC/EACTS 2025; ASE prosthetic valve 2024 | Stable; changed gradient; new regurgitation; no baseline; post-TAVI                    |
| `valve.procedure-strategy`       | Which factors should the Heart Team weigh?                             | Age/life expectancy, anatomy/access, prior surgery, concomitant CAD, comorbidity, frailty, preference and expertise; never output an autonomous procedure choice.    | ESC/EACTS 2025                            | TAVI/SAVR factors; repair/replacement; TEER; tricuspid options                         |

The candidate definitions store clinical question, inputs, trigger, exclusions, intended result, evidence metadata, boundary cases and governance state. Recommendation class/level is intentionally blank until independent licensed review confirms the exact attributable statement.
