# Hypertension: sources and implementation

11 October flow correction: acute BP assessment uses the existing severe-BP rule; outpatient confirmation and resistant-treatment assessment have one primary route. Resistance approval is tied to BP, out-of-office measurements and the medication regimen separately from subsequent secondary-cause/anatomical reports. Completing those reports advances RDN eligibility without repeating unchanged adherence/dose review; changed BP/treatment still invalidates it. Verified candidates can see a pending RDN assessment with missing checks, while actual scheduling retains all existing clinical, renal, secondary, anatomy, IFU and specialist/shared-decision requirements. Positive or uninterpretable aldosteronism screens and identified secondary disease do not become ready RDN candidates. Equivalent future hypertension tests and BP reviews are reused; overdue bookings and distinct annual surveillance are not treated as adequate substitutes. No guideline threshold or drug regimen was changed.

13 October activation policy: automatic confirmation/treatment and secondary-screening offers use clinician-recorded **Newly diagnosed** hypertension or current apparent resistance. Established or unspecified non-resistant hypertension uses the BP-target route. The record-entry date is not treated as the diagnosis date. Manual pathways remain available for other guideline indications; known abnormal screening reports and acute BP findings retain their review. BP goals cover both components, retain individualization and never combine measurements from separate times.

Reviewed 11 October 2026. ESC is the default framework. The latest dedicated ESC hypertension guideline remains 2024; the 2026 ESC cardiovascular disease/chronic kidney disease guideline updates BP management in CKD. The 2025 Endocrine Society guideline supplies primary aldosteronism screening recommendations. US hypertension thresholds are not mixed into the ESC defaults.

## Workflow boundaries

The 90-day freshness limit for BP/renal eligibility evidence is an application workflow constraint, not an ESC treatment cutoff. Prior decision clearance is tied to the reviewed observations, reports, regimen and diagnoses; new/corrected evidence or aging beyond this window reopens assessment. A booked procedure or completed questionnaire alone does not establish suitability.

Exact current regional Symplicity/Paradise instructions were not verified individually. Anatomy dimensions, vessel exclusions, device contraindications and peri-procedure drugs require review of the selected current IFU by the treating specialist; no universal anatomical cutoff or regimen is encoded. Secondary-cause clearance and a negative PA screen require documented specialist interpretation, not inference from absence of a diagnosis. Pregnancy and acute illness exit routine elective RDN care. The existing severe-BP pathway continues to assess acute organ injury; routine outpatient optimization is excluded during an open admission.

All content uses existing studies, procedures, decisions and dated plans. There is no separate hypertension patient store. New `home_bp` and `hypertension_workup` study kinds extend the existing study table in migration 014; matching hypertension plans are completed only by the appropriate report purpose.
