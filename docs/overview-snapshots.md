# Overview lab and medication snapshots

These are descriptive views of `cf.observation` and `cf.medication_event`. Clinical resolution, alerts, prescription actions, audit records and database schema are unchanged.

1. Latest lab value: the newest non-withdrawn measurement, with its actual date and preliminary badge when relevant. If it differs from the value selected by the clinical resolver, both are labelled. The trend compares the newest verified numeric result to the nearest strictly earlier verified result in the same normalized unit. It uses `(latest − previous) / abs(previous) × 100`; a zero baseline shows the absolute change with an explanation on expansion. First values, preliminary values and duplicate collection timestamps do not produce a percentage. Corrections are versions of a measurement, not a new baseline. Direction is neutral; a downward line does not mean improvement.
2. Medication history: recorded starts/restarts, doses, frequency changes, holds, not-taking, resumption, stops and continuation, ordered by effective date. Expand a row for the prior/new regimen and recorded reason. Withdrawn/corrected entries follow the existing state resolver. A planned event does not replace an active regimen in replay.
3. HF dose progress: the prescribed total daily dose divided by the total daily reference dose. Only supported medicines, known frequencies, matching units and oral routes receive a percentage. No HF diagnosis, loop diuretics, unknown doses and PRN regimens do not acquire a fabricated target. A held/not-taken medicine's bar is striped and denotes its prescribed reference comparison. Bars cap at 100 visually, while the numeric label can exceed 100. These percentages are not instructions to titrate or indicators of tolerated/personal treatment completeness.

Reference doses and frequencies are grounded in [ESC HF 2021 Table 8](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/42/36/3599/6358045), also indexed in the [2026 ESC HF guideline](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/advance-article/doi/10.1093/eurheartj/ehag100/8766302). Primary indexed table text was checked on 11 October 2026; direct full-text opening was blocked by the publisher. The existing catalogue chooses one reference within ranges (for example enalapril 20 mg BID and spironolactone 50 mg OD). Ramipril is displayed as 5 mg BID, equivalent to the catalogue's total 10 mg daily. Carvedilol retains the catalogue's 25 mg BID reference; an individual weight/tolerance-dependent target may differ. No newly guessed dose or threshold is introduced.

Finerenone reuses the existing [Kerendia SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/13437/smpc) resolver (HF target 20/40 mg dependent on renal data). Missing/old/preliminary renal data leaves its percentage unavailable. Historical timeline percentages for finerenone are omitted because today's renal value cannot establish the historical target.

Visual checks cover phone widths 320/390 px, desktop 1440 px, expandable histories, keyboard-native details and reduced-motion preference. These do not establish physical iPhone/Safari behavior or full accessibility compliance.
