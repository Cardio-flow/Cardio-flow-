# Pregnancy and heart disease — evidence and scope

Verified 19 October 2026. This slice coordinates pre-conception, pregnancy, delivery planning and postpartum cardiac care through the existing condition, recommendation, decision and dated-plan kernel. It is not an autonomous obstetric prescriber, a full mWHO/CARPREG calculator, or a mechanical-valve anticoagulant dose calculator.

## Primary references

- [ESC pregnancy 2025](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/46/43/4462/8234487), DOI 10.1093/eurheartj/ehaf193: Table 6; sections 4–5, 12.3 and 12.6; Tables 14–15. Risk class is clinician-assigned. Missing risk assessment, moderate/severe ventricular impairment, significant functional limitation and disease-specific concerns direct care to a Pregnancy Heart Team. Class IV supports individualized counselling and reproductive autonomy. Fetal echo referral is offered for parental congenital disease, usually at 18–22 weeks. Delivery mode is documented, never inferred from a diagnosis alone.
- [Correction published February 2026](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/47/7/812/8400706), DOI 10.1093/eurheartj/ehaf1011: indexed correction changes the Figure 15 verapamil dose legend to 2.5–10 mg. This module contains no intravenous verapamil dosing. Primary article content and the correction's indexed text were accessible; direct ESC slide PDF returned 403 and correction HTML could not be retrieved in full. No claim of full PDF review.
- [ESC 2026 patient guidance announcement](https://www.escardio.org/news/press/press-releases/esc-launches/) reinforces shared decisions and coordinated care across the pregnancy journey. The [ESC 2026 HF guideline](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/47/38/5346/8766302) continues to refer detailed pregnancy management to ESC 2025.

## Implemented clinical boundaries

Current pregnancy/postpartum BP at or above 160 systolic **or** 110 diastolic prompts urgent assessment and repeat measurement. A new severe preliminary reading remains a verification signal. Possible pre-eclampsia prompts assessment of timing and new organ dysfunction; it does not create that diagnosis or assume that CKD is new. Pregnancy BP goals replace the adult default; routine adult RDN and BP-optimization routes are withheld. Dates and symptoms remain dated; an old observation is not copied forward as current.

Aspirin prophylaxis eligibility follows ESC: at least one high-risk or two moderate-risk factors, with confirmed dating in the routine starting window from week 12. Catalogue choices remain within 75–150 mg. Obstetric bleeding/allergy review and an exact indication/stop-review date are required. Existing aspirin or concurrent anticoagulant/P2Y12 treatment prevents a duplicate generic regimen. No aspirin or anticoagulant is automatically stopped. An existing Heart Team plan can be confirmed without creating another referral.

## Medication and laboratory integration

- Pregnancy and lactation differ. Compatible lactation options are retained. Contraindicated/not-recommended pregnancy drugs generate one integrated medication-safety finding; booking a review alone does not clear an unsafe current regimen. Generic pathway starts and dose changes cannot silently bypass pregnancy checks. Manual medication entry retains the clinical record and displays review warnings.
- [FDA statin communication, 2021](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/fda-requests-removal-strongest-warning-against-using-cholesterol-lowering-statins-during-pregnancy): most pregnancies warrant interruption, but selected very-high-risk cases require individualized decisions. No blanket claim that every statin exposure is an absolute contraindication. Lactation is considered separately.
- [FDA NSAID pregnancy advice](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/fda-recommends-avoiding-use-nsaids-pregnancy-20-weeks-or-later-because-they-can-result-low-amniotic): gestational restrictions protect against routine NSAID/high-dose-aspirin suggestions; low-dose obstetric prophylaxis is a separate indication.
- [EMA Wegovy product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wegovy-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 4.6: product-specific pre-conception counselling, including semaglutide washout. Lactation advice remains agent/formulation-specific; [LactMed tirzepatide, August 2026](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK581488/) does not support a universal incretin-class prohibition during breastfeeding.
- [WHO 2024 Hb thresholds](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK602185/table/ch5.tab2/) and [severity table](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK602183/table/fm-ch1.tab2/): pregnancy Hb limits 11/10.5/11 g/dL and severe anaemia below 7. [ACOG trimester definitions](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/how-your-fetus-grows-during-pregnancy) determine trimester at **sample collection**, from confirmed obstetric dating. Missing dating requests interpretation rather than applying the adult female threshold. The specialist treatment scope is prefilled; first-trimester or undated IV iron cannot use a generic adult route.
- [Renal Association pregnancy guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6822421/), 4.1.1: renal assessment uses serum creatinine; eGFR is not validated in pregnancy. The pregnancy medication review omits a calculated-clearance validation badge and explains the limitation. Existing dated laboratory records are retained. Adult HF reference-dose percentages and generic diabetes-target validation are not presented as pregnancy treatment goals.

Actual blood/study results close matching performance and retain the existing explicit results-review loop. Referrals and specialist reviews require documented clinical completion. Same-date/purpose appointments and equivalent future tests are reused. No separate tables, retrospective edits, automatic delivery or automatic pregnancy diagnosis from age/sex are introduced.
