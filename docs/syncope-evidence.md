# Syncope module implementation brief

Reference identities verified 17 Oct 2026: ESC syncope 2018 (PMID 29562304, DOI 10.1093/eurheartj/ehy037), practical instructions (29562291); ESC pacing 2021 (34455430). No newer dedicated adult ESC syncope guideline was found in the focused PubMed search. Open-access guideline comparison PMC7252783 reviewed for initial history, examination, orthostatic BP and ECG, risk-based disposition, directed testing, monitoring, reflex/OH treatment and avoidance of routine neurological imaging/EEG. Review PMID 42555934 (NEJM 2026) and ASPIRED randomized trial PMID 42670977 (NEJMoa2605812) abstracts verified. ASPIRED did not significantly reduce recurrent syncope at one year: no blanket 14-day monitor recommendation. EHRA cardioneuroablation 2024 (39082698) exists; no default ablation, pacing or device prescription.

Adults: trigger from explicit syncope/presyncope or unexplained fall recorded in the current/latest clinical context. Use the same recommendation → clinician decision → dated plan → matching result → review loop. Preserve traceability per context and relevant input signature; a new event or ECG reopens assessment even with an older reviewed episode.

Capture only remaining history: event type; circumstances/prodrome; recurrence frequency; red flags (none explicit); ECG review; orthostatic result; suspected mechanism; targeted tests; site of care; manual follow-up date. Do not prefill symptom denial. Known serious AV block/device/structural findings are shown as facts, never treated as causal without assessment. Do not permit a routine outpatient plan with current red flags or incomplete core assessment. Emergency action is a plan for assessment, not a diagnosis or autonomous admission.

Reuse ECG, Holter, echo and device checks. Add orthostatic BP as a structured investigation through the existing study kernel (supine, standing 1/3-min BP/HR, symptoms, reporter interpretation). No auto-OH diagnosis from incomplete measurements. Symptom frequency guides consideration of Holter vs longer monitoring/ILR consultation, with clinician selection. Structural concern/exertional events route to directed echo/valve/HCM assessment, not blanket stress tests. No automatic medicine changes; hypotensive/rate-slowing drugs are displayed for review, respecting HF/CKD constraints on fluid/salt advice.

Plan: dated emergency/specialist review, initial ECG/standing BP when missing, selected studies with their existing completion types, targeted counselling and driving/local-regulation advice. Result arrival requires review; a new significant result creates a fresh recommendation. Paediatric cases require a separate specialist route. No new tables.
